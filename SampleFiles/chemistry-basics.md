# Chemistry Basics

## Q&A
Q: What is the atomic number of carbon?
A: 6

Q: What is a covalent bond?
A: A bond formed when two atoms **share** a pair of electrons.

**Q:** Name three noble gases.
**A:**
- Helium
- Neon
- Argon

## Common ions
| Ion | Name | Charge |
|-----|------|--------|
| Na⁺ | Sodium | +1 |
| Cl⁻ | Chloride | −1 |
| SO₄²⁻ | Sulfate | −2 |
