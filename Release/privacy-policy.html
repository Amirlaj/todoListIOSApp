Flashies privacy policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026.

Your study library

Flashies stores modules, chapters, flashcards, tags, favourites and review history locally on your device. Export and import chapters or modules to move study material between devices. Flashies does not operate a server for your study content or automatically synchronize your library.

Files you choose

The app reads files you select for import. Exported chapter and module files contain your card text and can include review history. You control where exported files are saved and who receives them. CSV exports contain card fronts and backs.

Reminders and preferences

Study reminders are optional local notifications. Their time and your other app preferences are stored on your device. You can turn reminders off in Flashies or change notification permission in iOS Settings.

Advertising and analytics

Flashies has no advertising, tracking SDKs, third-party analytics or AI service integration. Apple may process App Store, TestFlight and device diagnostics according to your Apple settings and Apple's privacy policy.

Keeping or deleting data

You can export chapters or modules to keep a copy of your study data. Deleting a card, chapter or module removes its associated data and review history from this device. Deleting the app also removes its local library. Exported copies remain wherever you saved or shared them until you delete those copies.

Support

If you contact support, the recipient receives the information you choose to send, such as your email address, message and attachments. Avoid including private study content unless it is needed to explain the issue.
