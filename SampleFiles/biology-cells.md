## What is a cell?
The smallest **structural and functional unit** of a living organism.

## What are the two main types of cells?
- **Prokaryotic** – no nucleus (bacteria, archaea)
- **Eukaryotic** – have a nucleus (plants, animals, fungi)

## Mitochondria
- Produce energy (ATP) through cellular respiration
- Have their own DNA
- Often called the *powerhouse of the cell*

## Cell membrane
A phospholipid bilayer that controls what enters and leaves the cell.

## Key Terms
- **Nucleus**: Holds the cell's genetic material (DNA).
- **Ribosome**: Builds proteins from amino acids.
- **Cytoplasm**: Jelly-like fluid that fills the cell.
